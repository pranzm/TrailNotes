The goal was not speed. It was rhythm. I kept the first climb deliberately quiet, letting the breathing settle before the trail opened into rolling dirt.

The useful lesson was simple: when the effort stays honest early, the final kilometers become a place to practice form instead of negotiate with fatigue.
